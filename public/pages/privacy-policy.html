Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you voluntarily provide when booking services or creating an account, including your name, email address, phone number, and vehicle details such as make, model, year, and VIN. This information is necessary to deliver and manage the automotive services you request.

We automatically collect certain data when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, and usage patterns such as pages visited and time spent on the site. This information helps us improve our website performance and user experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used to deliver the automotive services you request, manage your appointments, maintain your service history, and communicate important updates about your vehicle. We may also use your information to send service reminders and seasonal maintenance recommendations.

We may use your contact information to respond to your inquiries, send booking confirmations, and provide post-service follow-ups. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols, secure server infrastructure, and strict access controls to protect your personal information. All payment processing is handled through PCI-DSS compliant systems, and our staff undergoes regular data security training to ensure your information remains confidential.

4. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to ensure basic functionality and remember your preferences. We also use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our site, which helps us improve content and navigation. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

We may share limited information with trusted third-party service providers who assist in operating our website, processing payments, and delivering communications. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it solely for the purposes we specify.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. You may also request a copy of the data we hold about you or withdraw consent for non-essential data processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information provided below.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us at help@carhive.co or call us at +1 (312) 555-0148. We will respond to all requests within 30 business days.
