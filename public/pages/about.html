OUR STORY

Batteries Fixed Where You Park

CAR HIVE started with one van, one load tester, and a stubborn idea: most flat batteries do not need a workshop, they need someone to come to them. Our first technician drove a hired van across Chicago fixing dead batteries in driveways and office car parks.

Today every van in the fleet carries lithium boosters, load testers, and OEM-spec batteries for the makes and models we see most. Same 60-minute promise, same fixed prices, same rule: you approve the price before any work starts.

We still test before we replace. Plenty of "dead batteries" are actually alternator faults or hidden electrical drains, and telling you the truth is the whole reason drivers keep our number.

Our Mission

To keep drivers moving with honest, fixed-price battery work wherever their car happens to be parked. We test before we replace, explain what we found in plain language, and never sell a battery you do not need.

Our Vision

To be the first number every driver calls when a battery goes flat — a company known for honest testing, punctual arrival, and pricing you were told before we turned a single bolt.
